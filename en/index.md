## What is U-Hermes?

**U-Hermes** is a Chinese-localized portable distribution of [NousResearch hermes-agent](https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent), shipped as either:

- A **Linux Live USB** image (Ventoy + Ubuntu 24.04 + persistence + hermes-agent), MIT.
- A **Windows portable USB drive** (commercial, ¥199+), with an Electron launcher and Xiapan Cloud account.

The English documentation is in early stage. **Most content is currently in [Chinese](/zh/)**.

## Translate this
